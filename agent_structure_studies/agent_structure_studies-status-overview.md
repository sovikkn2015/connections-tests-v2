## What Is Agentblazer Status?

Agentblazer Status is a Trailhead-based recognition program that provides a structured learning path for mastering AI and Agentforce. It guides learners through building autonomous agents and showcases their expertise in the digital workforce era.

The program is structured into three progressive levels — Champion, Innovator, and Legend — each with dedicated Trails containing modules, hands-on challenges, badges, and points. Your status is displayed on your Trailblazer Profile.

## Why It Exists

Salesforce introduced Agentblazer Status to:

- Provide a clear answer to "how do I get started with Agentforce?"
- Encourage AI adoption across industries
- Recognize professionals who excel in AI-driven automation
- Build a community of AI experts who can help businesses transition
- Create new career opportunities in AI and automation

As Leah McGowen-Hare (SVP, Trailblazer Community) put it: digital labor isn't your competition — it's your collaboration partner. The question isn't *if* you'll work with AI, but *how*.

## The Three Levels at a Glance

| Level | Title | Focus | Effort |
|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Champion** | Foundational AI & Agentforce concepts, build your first agent | ~7 hrs, 6,300 pts |
| 2 | **Innovator** | Strategy, use cases, deploy Agentforce for Sales & Service | ~12 hrs, 10,300 pts |
| 3 | **Legend** | Full agent lifecycle, advanced customization, Agentforce Specialist Cert | ~19 hrs, 16,900 pts |

## 2026 Changes: Annual Renewal Model

In 2026, Agentblazer Status shifted to a year-based recognition model:

- Statuses are now earned annually (similar to a living certification)
- New badges are added to each trail to keep learning aligned with the latest innovations
- Past statuses (e.g., 2025) remain permanently visible on your Trailblazer Profile
- Expect additional new badges may be required when renewing in future years

## Agentforce Specialist Certification

Legend Status (Level 3) is the first and only Agentblazer designation to include an actual Salesforce Certification — the Agentforce Specialist. This is a proctored, multiple-choice exam available virtually or in-person. The first attempt was offered free through the end of 2025.

## Getting Started

1. Go to [trailhead.salesforce.com/agentblazer](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/agentblazer)
2. Start with the **Become an Agentblazer Champion** trail
3. Levels unlock sequentially — complete Champion before Innovator, Innovator before Legend
4. Each completed module earns badges and points toward your status
